# DEADNET
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Delegated explicitly in the master brief: local-first TypeScript app, React/Vite for presentation, Node orchestration, SQLite persistence, one shared local open-weight model. The full local backend can use OpenRouter for hosted open-weight inference or Ollama for measured local inference. The public Pages edition is explicitly scripted.

## Users
Hackathon judges and curious visitors exploring a fictional abandoned computer. They need a compelling first minute, a discoverable investigation, and verifiable engineering evidence.

## Product Purpose
The internet survived humanity. Nobody told the accounts. Investigate three synthetic people whose histories disagree; decide whether to expose the archive or preserve their comforting fiction.

## Positioning
An evidence-routing social simulation: a visitor decides who learns an archival fact. Model-generated interpretations become bounded public or private events, change relationships, and propagate through the network. The investigation is inspectable outside chat.

## Operating Context
Runs on a 16 GB Apple Silicon Mac, with local inference after installation. Fictional timestamps remain separate from real development dates. New project begins October 29, 2026. Contest deadline October 31, 23:59 PDT (November 1, 12:29 IST).

## Capabilities and Constraints
Three accounts, finite autonomous turns, bounded memories and generations, lasting SQLite consequences, archive search, contextual file sharing, accessible desktop windows. Local inference is foundational. Deterministic baseline is explicitly labeled. One ElevenLabs voicemail and cached playback are verified. Current free OpenRouter capacity is throttled; do not claim a successful free hosted run. No paid service or external publication of contest entry without final user approval.

## Brand Commitments
DEADNET; late-1990s desktop with credited Fugue PNG icons, a sourced forest wallpaper and optional plain teal desktop, gray beveled windows, quiet psychological horror. No Microsoft artwork, real deceased people, repetitive jump scares, generic dashboard, or infinite agent swarm. User delegates design and implementation decisions and requests no further intervention except credentials, payments, ownership, irreversible external actions.

## Evidence on Hand
Empty workspace at start. GitHub CLI authenticated as sea-deep. No model runner or ElevenLabs environment key found initially. Benchmarks and screenshots must come from the working build.

## Product Principles
- Simulation owns state; dialogue proposes constrained actions.
- Knowledge is scoped: private conversations stay private.
- Every generated consequence has inspectable provenance.
- Small society, complete investigation, honest limits.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Sound only after an explicit power gesture, persisted mute, reduced motion, keyboard controls, readable text, mobile single-window mode. Fictional horror without impersonation.
